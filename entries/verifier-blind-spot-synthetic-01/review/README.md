## What a review record should contain

A reviewer who is not the entry's author adds one file here, named
`<reviewer>-<date>.md`. The file records:

- the reviewer's name, as they identify themselves, and the date;
- the commit of scientific-evidence-gate and the corpus revision reviewed;
- whether the fixture isolates the stated defect and nothing else;
- whether the expected outcome follows from the tool's documented
  contract, rather than from its observed behaviour;
- whether the run was reproduced locally, and the result;
- any disagreement, left unresolved rather than smoothed over.

Only then is the reviewer added to `provenance.reviewed_by`.
